The whole routeStep 0 of 7

Read the route as text
  1. You bring a question. Sources enter when supplied or retrieved. The models run on your hardware.
  2. The Analyst builds the first draft from the question, reasoning and available evidence.
  3. The Humanist also reads that draft, possibly shortened, and adds who is affected, what matters and what it overlooks.
  4. The Skeptic also reads both earlier drafts and argues against their strongest agreement, not against you.
  5. One synthesized draft combines the perspectives. Synthesis normally reuses the Analyst's model, so it is not an independent fourth judge.
  6. Accept, question or reject the draft. The decision stays with you.
  7. Agreement is not proof. This illustrates the standard full council route, not a live run. Timing is not to scale.

The optional extra cross-critique round is off by default. A separate debate mode can use additional rounds. Modes, settings and model availability can change the stages.
