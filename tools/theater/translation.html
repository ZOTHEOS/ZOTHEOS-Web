Language And Translation
Translate The Whole Page

Use your browser's Translate Page option. On a phone, look in the browser menu. On desktop Chrome, right-click the page and choose Translate. Available languages depend on your browser.

Video audio and captions are separate. Use each video's own caption controls when available; ZOTHEOS does not translate the recording.

Translate A Reading Section

Choose the selected story, the Theater introduction or the curation notes, then choose your language in Google Translate.

Choose Language In Google Translate ↗

This opens Google and sends only the selected public reading text when you press the link. Searches and form entries are excluded. Automatic translations may change names or meaning. Check the original sources. Without page scripts, copy a passage into Google Translate yourself.
