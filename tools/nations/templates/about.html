How this list is made.

Indigenous nations today names one nation at a time, as the nation names itself, with checked facts and the source of each one.

The method

Each entry starts from the nation's own name, taken from the nation's own site where one exists, otherwise from a national registry, a representative body or another published source. Every fact is written in our own words from a published page, and carries the page's publisher, a link to it, and a short phrase copied from it that supports the fact. A fact without a supporting phrase is not published.

Sources come in order: the nation's own pages first, then governments, national registries and statistics offices, then UN bodies and international organizations, then museums, universities and research. Wikipedia is never cited.

The checking tool

A tool named {{user_agent}} re-reads every cited page each week. It says who it is, obeys each site's robots.txt, sends at most one request to a site every three seconds and never pretends to be a browser. If the phrase is gone, the fact is hidden until a person restores it. If a site refuses the tool, its facts follow the waiting and last-successful-check rules. A person check is shown only when a dated receipt exists. The full record is on the verification page.

The rotation

The ring is seeded by the date, the same for everyone. The walk is seeded by a key kept only in your browser. Each day, fifteen nations, one from each region, form the ring; after that each device walks on alone and does not repeat a nation within a region until the region is exhausted.

Editorial Responsibility

ZOTHEOS prepares these pages from published sources. Automated evidence checks and separate source and meaning reviews have different roles. A source review is not a person check; any person check needs its own dated receipt.

No requests to other sites

Open your browser's network panel on any page here: every request goes to zotheos.org. There are no analytics, no embeds, no outside fonts and no images from other sites. Sources open only when you follow them.

Typefaces
