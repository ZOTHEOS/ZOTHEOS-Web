In the count of days

Today in the tonalpohualli, the count of days. Its 260 days turn 13 numbers with 20 day signs, so each pairing returns once in every 260 days.

The count is a living tradition: K’iche’ Maya daykeepers in highland Guatemala keep their own 260-day count, the cholq’ij, today. Here it is shown only as a calendar; ZOTHEOS does not read fortunes from it.

The number is written with dots, as the codices write it, one dot for each count. The rings are added here to show the rest of the thirteen, and the dots are grouped in fives for reading. Computed in your browser with the Caso correlation, in which 13 August 1521 (Julian; 23 August in today’s calendar) was 1 Coatl, Serpent. The day turns at your local midnight.

How one answer is forged.

One question, three perspectives, a decision that stays yours. In the standard full council, each mind builds on the drafts before it. Synthesis brings their reasoning together without making agreement a guarantee of truth.

Read the route as text
  1. You bring a question. Sources enter when supplied or retrieved. The models run on your hardware.
  2. The Analyst builds the first draft from the question, reasoning and available evidence.
  3. The Humanist also reads that draft, possibly shortened, and adds who is affected, what matters and what it overlooks.
  4. The Skeptic also reads both earlier drafts and argues against their strongest agreement, not against you.
  5. One synthesized draft combines the perspectives. Synthesis normally reuses the Analyst's model, so it is not an independent fourth judge.
  6. Accept, question or reject the draft. The decision stays with you.
  7. Agreement is not proof. This illustrates the standard full council route, not a live run. Timing is not to scale.

The optional extra cross-critique round is off by default. A separate debate mode can use additional rounds. Modes, settings and model availability can change the stages.

Method, research and limits

What the route means. Later minds receive earlier drafts as context; that context can be shortened. Synthesis normally reuses the Analyst's model with a synthesis prompt, rather than adding an independent fourth judge. Question-based emphasis is prompt guidance, not a measured competence score. A separate debate mode can use additional rounds.

Research context. Du et al., Improving Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate studies models exchanging and revising answers on selected tasks. It motivates testing deliberation; it does not establish ZOTHEOS's accuracy.

Why confidence needs care. Kadavath et al., Language Models (Mostly) Know What They Know studies model self-evaluation and its limits. ZOTHEOS also uses heuristic quality signals. A stated confidence level is not automatically a calibrated probability. Models can share mistakes, and additional discussion cannot guarantee a correct answer.

Proof

Built to be checked.

ZOTHEOS includes signed result receipts, local forecast records, evidence-linked corrections and backend network checks. They make the work inspectable, and their desktop integration is part of the current validation.

01

Signed receipts

Verify result-receipt signatures offline. A signature check establishes whether the receipt matches its signing key; it does not prove the answer is correct.

02

Prediction ledger

Forecasts the system logged, scored against what actually happened. A local track record with a reliability diagram.

03

Belief drift

When the council changes its mind, the full chain stays: v1 to v2 to v3, with the evidence that moved it. No memory-holing.

04

Corrections in the open

Every flagged correction is kept with provenance in an immutable log, and approved or rejected where you can see it.

05

Backend network checks

A backend network ledger and canary check make backend controls inspectable. They do not establish that every desktop-shell or WebView2 connection is blocked.

Mission

Give power back
to humanity.

Truth, dignity and sovereignty guide ZOTHEOS, especially in service to people whose voices have been silenced or overlooked. The purpose is to help people examine evidence, strengthen their own judgment and act with care for one another and the living world. You remain free to question the answer, disagree and decide.

Truth with evidence. Dignity without exception. Judgment that remains yours.

Universal peace is the moral horizon for ZOTHEOS. The work begins with accessible knowledge, respect for human rights and the freedom to think without coercion. Accountability requires evidence, fair processes and the dignity of every person.

Evolved Intelligence is a research direction toward accountable learning, useful self-correction and understanding. It is not a claim of consciousness, infallibility or absolute truth.

Care for people includes care for land, water and the ecosystems that sustain life. Responsible computation requires assessing energy, hardware and resource costs honestly. Local AI has a footprint too; environmental benefit must be measured, not assumed.

Evidence before certaintyThe standard: show sources, preserve uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.

Frequently asked

Questions worth asking.

Does ZOTHEOS actually work offline?
Local inference runs via llama.cpp once the required models are available. Model downloads and optional network features need a connection. The local edition stores conversations and vault files on your device.
Why three models instead of one larger one?
Analyst, Humanist, and Skeptic examine questions from different angles, then synthesize an answer that can expose evidence and disagreement. Multiple models can share the same error, so agreement is not proof.
What hardware do I need?
ZOTHEOS is being developed for broad hardware access, including desktops, laptops, mini PCs, older computers and kiosks. Current desktop release testing focuses on Windows x64. Processor support, memory, storage and model compatibility still matter; compact-system startup and other platforms remain in development.
Is any of my data collected?
The local edition stores conversations, vault and memory on the device running it. This website sends waitlist emails to Formspree for milestone updates, and loads hosted fonts and media. Its Theater section contacts YouTube and the National Film Board of Canada as that section approaches the screen. Local inference and website processing are separate.
How is ZOTHEOS funded?
Development is independent and self-funded, with optional support. The local edition is free for individual use. Support does not unlock features, access, priority or influence. Institutional deployment and support may be paid; they are not required for individual local use.
What is the “evidence vault”?
A local, tiered knowledge base the council can cite. Retrieval can rerank sources, and strict mode checks grounding and citation tags. A citation still needs to be inspected against its source.
How do I know it is really offline?
The backend includes a network ledger and a canary check for its network controls. Those checks cover backend activity, not every shell or WebView2 connection. Receipt signatures can be checked offline, separately from network behavior.
What happens when ZOTHEOS is wrong?
Flagged corrections are recorded with provenance for review. Belief-drift records retain evidence linked to a change. These records make changes inspectable; they do not prove that an answer is correct.
Can kids use ZOTHEOS?
Kids Mode is not enabled in the current product. Age-band support remains planned.
What is the Smart Board?
An AI whiteboard with council prompts, document tools, charts, voice and conversation saving. Available functions depend on the configured models and tools.
Can I choose my own models?
Yes. Model Setup browses Hugging Face and imports local GGUF files, so starter packs are a beginning rather than a fixed catalog. Compatible chat models can be assigned to the council roles. Each model retains its own license, and runtime support plus available memory determine what can run. GitHub files can be imported locally when compatible; an integrated GitHub downloader is not established.
From the founder

I build and maintain ZOTHEOS as a one-person independent project.

I built ZOTHEOS because I was tired of tools that treat attention as the product and truth as optional. The internet learned to keep people scrolling; I wanted something that helps people think instead.

Roots and gratitude

ZOTHEOS draws its visual language from ancient Mesoamerica: the stepped fret, the fine black line of the painted codices, the count of days. It honors the Indigenous peoples of the Americas: the Nahua, Maya, Zapotec and many other peoples of Mexico and Central America, and the Native nations further north. They are distinct peoples with connected roots that are still living, and many Mexican and Mexican American families carry those roots too. As the project grows, it intends to work with Indigenous scholars and community members, so that every symbol here is used accurately and with permission. Any wider work with the traditions of other peoples is meant to follow the same rule: their guidance, their permission. That work has a first page: Indigenous nations today, their own names first, every fact with its source and the date it was checked.

Sources and orthography

Nahuatl is a living language, spoken today in Nahua communities across Mexico. Words on this site use one spelling, the classical unmarked orthography, written without vowel-length or saltillo marks (Quiahuitl, Rain; Ollin, Movement; Ozomatli, Monkey); modern Nahua writers often use other conventions. Each word is glossed in English where it appears.

The day in the header and below is computed in your browser with the Caso correlation, in which 13 August 1521 (Julian; 23 August in today’s calendar) was 1 Coatl, Serpent. It is shown as a date only, never as a horoscope.

The stepped fret, xicalcoliuhqui (“twisted gourd”), appears in the Zapotec stone mosaics of Mitla and on the shields painted in the Codex Mendoza; the version here is redrawn from its construction, not traced from any single work. The line follows the fine black contour line of the painted codices. For the language itself, see the Online Nahuatl Dictionary (Wired Humanities Projects).
