02 / Architecture

Three minds. One grounded answer.

Like a medical conference—multiple specialists consult, compare perspectives, then deliver one careful recommendation. ZOTHEOS brings that logic to intelligence.

⚖

Fusion architecture

Three local AI models—Analyst, Humanist, and Skeptic—each examine your query from a different angle. Their perspectives are scored, compared, and synthesized into a single verdict with transparent confidence scoring. You see where they agreed, where they clashed, and why.

⍑

Local-first

Runs on your hardware. No cloud dependency. Your conversations, memory, and knowledge never leave your device unless you choose otherwise.

◆

Evidence vault

A curated local knowledge base with tiered sourcing—treaties, regulations, and peer-reviewed work ranked above opinion. Grounded answers, not hallucinations.

✓

Bias detection

Every response is scored for bias level, emotional charge, and reliability spread. Absolutist framing and loaded language get flagged automatically.

★

Model transparency

See exactly which model said what. GGUF files, quantization levels, inference parameters—everything disclosed. No black boxes. No hidden reasoning. Every claim attributed to the core that produced it, with cross-core agreement scores visible.

Data brokers are also subject to the CCPA. Organizations must implement robust data protection policies and provide clear transparency about data usage and algorithms.
