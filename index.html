02 / Architecture

Three minds.
One grounded answer.

Like a medical conference—multiple specialists consult, compare perspectives, then deliver one careful recommendation.

⚖

Fusion architecture

Three local AI models—Analyst, Humanist, and Skeptic—each examine your query from a different angle. Their perspectives are scored, compared, and synthesized into a single verdict with transparent confidence scoring.

◉

Local-first

Runs on your hardware. No cloud dependency. Your conversations, memory, and knowledge never leave your device.

◆

Evidence vault

A curated local knowledge base with tiered sourcing—treaties, regulations, and peer-reviewed work ranked above opinion.

✓

Bias detection

Every response scored for bias level, emotional charge, and reliability. Absolutist framing and loaded language get flagged.

★

Model transparency

See exactly which model said what. GGUF files, quantization levels, inference parameters—everything disclosed. No black boxes. Every claim attributed to the core that produced it.
