How We Curate

Attention With Responsibility.

Distinct stories, official sources and honest viewing notes. No film stands for every Nation.

Read Our Curation And Historical Context

We read official filmmaker, publisher and community sources before adding a title. Credits, source-check dates and viewing limits remain with each story. Inclusion is not an endorsement by a Nation, independent verification of every statement or a claim that the full recording was watched for this collection.

Trailers, excerpts and recorded presentations are labeled. Official viewing pages may require payment, an account or access from a particular country. Availability can change. Videos remain with their authorized providers; ZOTHEOS does not copy or host them.

History In Context

The collection includes the official Daughter of a Lost Bird excerpt on Indigenous adoption. Its child-removal percentage is historical testimony about research before the Indian Child Welfare Act, not a present-day removal rate. Read NICWA’s Historical Context.

This growing collection is incomplete. Corrections, living cultures, community-led restoration and careful source review guide its growth.

Theater Privacy

Fonts and page code load from this site. Theme and motion preferences stay in your browser. The Theater does not save searches or film selections.

Official thumbnails load automatically from their listed image providers. The selected YouTube, PBS, Vimeo or National Film Board player also loads automatically, ready for its Play control. These providers receive your IP address and request information when contacted; their own privacy policies apply. No autoplay permission is granted.

YouTube uses its privacy-enhanced player domain and Vimeo requests its do-not-track mode. Neither makes playback anonymous or guarantees that a provider collects no data. Unload Player removes the frame. Loading or selecting another available player contacts its provider again.

Source links contact their destinations when followed. Publishers can restrict or remove a title. If playback fails, use the official source. Report A Correction.
