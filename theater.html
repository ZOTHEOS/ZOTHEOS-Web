How We Curate

Attention With
Responsibility.

We read official filmmaker, publisher and community sources before adding a title. Credits, source-check dates and viewing limits stay beside it. Inclusion is not an endorsement by a Nation, an independent verification of every statement in a film or a claim that the full film has been watched for this collection.

Trailers and excerpts are labeled. Official pages may require payment, a library account or access in a particular country. Availability can change. Full films remain with their authorized providers; ZOTHEOS does not copy or host them.

Difficult histories belong beside living cultures, relationships, resistance and community-led restoration. This starter collection is incomplete. Sources and corrections guide its growth.

Theater Privacy

The page loads its artwork, fonts and code from this site. Theme and motion preferences stay in your browser. Search and film selection are not saved by the Theater.

A video provider is contacted only after you press Load Player for a selected title. Loading shares connection and request information with that provider. YouTube uses its privacy-enhanced embed domain; this does not make playback anonymous or eliminate provider data collection. PBS uses its own player. No player is loaded automatically and no autoplay permission is granted.

External source and provider-policy links contact their destinations when followed. A publisher can restrict or remove a video. If a player does not work, use its official source. Report A Correction.
