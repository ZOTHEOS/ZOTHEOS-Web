PATHWAYS IN THIS EDITION

Land, Water And Restoration
Prepare: A source-linked land-and-water brief with one requested decision, its decision-maker, evidence gaps and a verified next date.
Reflect: Who will hold authority, access and responsibility after the proposed agreement, and who gets to say no?

Representation With Decision-Making Power
Prepare: A decision map connecting one issue to the institution that can act, the participation route, a verified date and a public accountability question.
Reflect: Would a person at the table have a meaningful say, resources and accountability to the people affected?

United Nations Declaration On The Rights Of Indigenous Peoples | https://cambodia.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/book/DRIP-En.pdf | Source checked 2026-10-16

Recover Records And Keep The Context
Prepare: An evidence ledger that separates sourced statements, interpretations, disputed accounts and unanswered questions.
Reflect: Could someone with a different view inspect the same evidence and see where your conclusion comes from?
American Indian Treaties: Supporting Documentation | https://www.archives.gov/research/native-americans/treaties/supporting-documentation | Source checked 2026-10-16

Language And Culture Under Community Direction
Prepare: A community-approved learning plan describing the audience, permitted materials, speaker review and access rules.
Reflect: Will this project strengthen speakers and learners while leaving the community in control of its knowledge?

Control Over Data And Digital Work
Prepare: A data-use agreement checklist with decision authority, purpose, access, retention, permitted reuse and an exit plan.
Reflect: Can the people whose knowledge or records are involved understand the use, change the rules and stop it?

Children, Families And Community Connections
Prepare: A private question list and document index for a qualified adviser, with verified dates and identifying information kept out of public material.
Reflect: Does the next step protect the child’s safety, relationships, privacy and the community’s lawful role?
What Is ICWA? | https://www.nicwa.org/what-is-icwa/ | Source checked 2026-10-16
