SOMETHING YOU CAN PREPARE
A community-directed commitment record: requested change, authorized decision-maker, resources, evidence, review date and a way to correct or stop.

WHO DECIDES AND WHAT TO VERIFY
1. Agree on what would count as progress and what would trigger revision or stopping. Record actual observations and uncertainty; do not invent participation, consent or outcomes.
2. For allies and institutions: ask what support is wanted, resource the work fairly and accept refusal. The guide is not a mandate to initiate a project in someone else’s name.

REFLECTION
If the people affected said this did not help, could the project change course and return control?
