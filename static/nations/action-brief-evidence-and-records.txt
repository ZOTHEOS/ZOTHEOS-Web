WHO DECIDES AND WHAT TO VERIFY
1. Archives may contain colonial language, omissions and harmful classifications. Explain their context instead of adopting those labels as fact.
2. Preserve original files and record corrections. Historical rolls, ancestry research and a search result do not determine present-day citizenship or membership.
