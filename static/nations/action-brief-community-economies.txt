WHO DECIDES AND WHAT TO VERIFY
1. Confirm all eligibility, cost, repayment, ownership and deadline details with the actual provider. This guide is not financial advice.
2. Ask who owns the result and carries the risk. A project should build community capacity rather than transfer control through unclear terms.

REFLECTION
