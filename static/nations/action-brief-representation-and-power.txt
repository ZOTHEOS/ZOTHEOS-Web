WHO DECIDES AND WHAT TO VERIFY
1. National representation does not replace a Nation’s own authority or consent. Who represents a Nation is for its own procedures, not ZOTHEOS, to decide.
2. Check current election and participation requirements. This guide is nonpartisan public information, not campaign or legal representation.
3. AFN’s membership account is not a complete inventory of Alaska Native peoples or governments. Verify the relevant Nation’s own account and the specific institution’s public rules before assigning decision-making authority.
