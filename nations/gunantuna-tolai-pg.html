Gunantuna

Also known as Tolai

Explore This Record

  • Lands

Lands

the Gazelle Peninsula of New Britain Island, East New Britain Province, Papua New Guinea

Countries Papua New Guinea

Facts 3

Achievement

In 2016 a Queensland gallery commissioned A Bit na Ta, a collaborative work made with the wider Tolai community whose music carries the rhythms of Tolai life from 1875 to 1975.

As of 2016 · Source: Queensland Art Gallery | Gallery of Modern Art, Art from Papua New Guinea Celebrated in Major QAG Exhibition (opens in a new tab) · Checked 8 Oct 2026 · Suggest a correction to this fact

Today

Diwarra pays for everyday things such as store goods and school fees, and sharing it out expresses ties between clans and individuals.

Source: Queensland Art Gallery | Gallery of Modern Art, Gunantuna (Tolai people) (opens in a new tab) · Checked 8 Oct 2026 · Suggest a correction to this fact

Culture

The Gunantuna use two currencies: Papua New Guinea's kina and toea, and tabu or diwarra, a shell money that is legal tender throughout East New Britain.

Source: Queensland Art Gallery | Gallery of Modern Art, Gunantuna (Tolai people) (opens in a new tab) · Checked 8 Oct 2026 · Suggest a correction to this fact

Roots and gratitude

ZOTHEOS draws its visual language from ancient Mesoamerica: the stepped fret, the fine black line of the painted codices, the count of days. It honors the Indigenous peoples of the Americas: the Nahua, Maya, Zapotec and many other peoples of Mexico and Central America, and the Native nations further north. They are distinct peoples with connected roots that are still living, and many Mexican and Mexican American families carry those roots too. As the project grows, it intends to work with Indigenous scholars and community members, so that every symbol here is used accurately and with permission. Any wider work with the traditions of other peoples is meant to follow the same rule: their guidance, their permission. That work has a first page: Indigenous nations today, their own names first, every fact with its source and the date it was checked.
