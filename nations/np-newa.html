Facts 3

Language

In 2021 Nepal's Language Commission recommended Nepal Bhasa, alongside Tamang, as an official language of Bagmati Province.

As of 2021 · Source: Radio Nepal, Language Commission recommends province-wise official languages before government (opens in a new tab) · Checked 8 Oct 2026 · Suggest a correction to this fact

Land

The Newa homeland is the Kathmandu Valley, called Swanigah in Nepal Bhasa; in 2021 more than half of Nepal's 1,341,363 Newa lived in its three districts.

As of 2021 · Source: National Foundation for Development of Indigenous Nationalities, Nepal, नेवार (Newar) (opens in a new tab) · Checked 8 Oct 2026 · Suggest a correction to this fact

Language

Nepal Bhasa, the Newa language, has its own scripts, Ranjana and Prachalit, along with its own grammar, dictionaries and literature.

Source: National Foundation for Development of Indigenous Nationalities, Nepal, नेवार (Newar) (opens in a new tab) · Checked 8 Oct 2026 · Suggest a correction to this fact

Roots and gratitude

ZOTHEOS draws its visual language from ancient Mesoamerica: the stepped fret, the fine black line of the painted codices, the count of days. It honors the Indigenous peoples of the Americas: the Nahua, Maya, Zapotec and many other peoples of Mexico and Central America, and the Native nations further north. They are distinct peoples with connected roots that are still living, and many Mexican and Mexican American families carry those roots too. As the project grows, it intends to work with Indigenous scholars and community members, so that every symbol here is used accurately and with permission. Any wider work with the traditions of other peoples is meant to follow the same rule: their guidance, their permission. That work has a first page: Indigenous nations today, their own names first, every fact with its source and the date it was checked.
