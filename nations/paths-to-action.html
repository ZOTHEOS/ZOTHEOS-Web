Truth · Dignity · Sovereignty

What Can ZOTHEOS Do For Indigenous People And Nations?

Make knowledge useful. Follow the evidence, identify a decision that matters and prepare a next step you control. Truth, dignity and sovereignty are the standard for judging the work.

This is a practical starting point for people, families, community organizations and Nations worldwide, including people outside government recognition lists. Each community chooses its own priorities and representation. No single story, institution or workflow stands for all Indigenous peoples.

Pathway 01 of 10

Land, Water And Restoration

A serious answer to land loss begins with the outcome the affected people want. UNDRIP addresses lands and resources in Article 26, redress including restitution in Article 28, and development priorities and consent in Article 32. These standards matter alongside the particular legal system and the Nation’s own institutions.

Prepare A Brief Sourced Examples Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Begin With The Nation’s Account

Use the directory and linked histories to identify the distinct Nation and its own published priorities. Follow the original source. A map, a state label or this directory cannot establish title, a complete homeland boundary or authority to negotiate.

Work You Control

Build A Decision Brief

For a public proposal affecting land or water, record the project, responsible authority, published documents, verified deadline and the community’s authorized position. Ask which outcome is sought: return of ownership, protection of access, restoration, a decision-making agreement or another remedy. Record these separately; an invitation to advise does not itself transfer ownership.

Proposed App Workflow

Organize Evidence For Human Review

A future, tested ZOTHEOS workflow could compare public project documents, extract dated commitments and draft questions with page references. It would need to preserve uncertainty and original files. Counsel, rights holders and authorized community representatives would assess the evidence and choose the remedy.

Pathway 02 of 10

Representation With Decision-Making Power

Indigenous people can seek influence in national and local institutions while their Nations sustain their own systems of government. UNDRIP Articles 5 and 18 recognize these distinct dimensions. More Indigenous officeholders can be a community goal; meaningful authority, accountability and chosen representation also need attention.

Prepare A Brief Sourced Examples Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Find Practical Voting Resources

For US elections, follow NARF’s voter resources for registration, identification, polling access, Native-language assistance and Tribal leaders’ guides. This site connects you to those resources; it does not register voters, endorse a party, recruit candidates or speak for a Nation.

Work You Control

Name The Decision And The Seat

Separate a public-election goal from a Nation’s own governance process. For the issue you choose, identify the council, school board, agency, legislature or other body with actual authority. Write the decision sought, who is affected and how representatives will report back. Verify current eligibility, deadlines and voting rules through official channels.

Proposed App Workflow

Compare Public Commitments

A proposed ZOTHEOS workflow could place published platforms, meeting records, budgets and later votes beside one another with exact citations. People would set the questions and check context. The system should not infer someone’s identity or political preferences, score a community’s loyalty or make an endorsement in its name.

Hypothetical reader question: How can I avoid treating different governing bodies as interchangeable when I study a public decision?

Pathway 03 of 10

Recover Records And Keep The Context

Historical work becomes more useful when another person can trace a statement back to its source. The US National Archives links treaty texts with supporting correspondence, council records and catalog identifiers. Its records are one institutional perspective; a Nation’s own accounts and protocols belong in the research plan.

Prepare A Brief Sourced Examples Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Follow Facts To Their Sources

Read a profile’s Facts And Sources and its deeper history links. Notice the difference between an event year and the date the source was checked. The directory’s coverage notes can reveal missing records without turning an omission into proof that a people did not exist.

Work You Control

Make An Evidence Ledger

For each statement, record the original title, author or institution, date, URL or archive identifier, page, supporting passage and what it does not establish. Keep conflicting accounts side by side. Ask a community archive or historian how to handle names and records before making a family or collective history public.

Proposed App Workflow

Draft With Verifiable References

A possible desktop workflow could help organize a permitted document collection into an evidence table and a timeline. It would need reliable page references and checks for missing or misread text. An extracted sentence is a starting point for review; it cannot decide whether a treaty remains applicable or a legal claim succeeds.

Pathway 04 of 10

Language And Culture Under Community Direction

A language project can begin with everyday learning and a community’s own goals. FPCC publishes planning, language-nest, family-learning and mentor-apprentice resources. Its FirstVoices tools support community-managed language sites. Availability of a tool or a public recording does not establish permission to copy, translate or train a model on it.

Prepare A Brief Sourced Examples Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Find Community-Led Learning

Use Nations’ own links and the FPCC resource guide to locate a suitable starting point. Read who the material is for and what reuse is allowed. This website’s translation guidance concerns public reading text; automated translation is not an authority on an Indigenous language.

Work You Control

Plan A Small Learning Project

With the relevant speakers and community authority, choose an audience, a modest learning goal and an approved set of materials. Agree on attribution, payment, access, reuse and the ability to withdraw material before recording. Choose an existing community-led resource when it already meets the need.

Proposed App Workflow

Support A Teacher-Checked Workflow

A future ZOTHEOS workflow might organize approved lesson notes or draft practice activities in a language it has been evaluated for. Fluent speakers would check every output. It must not invent translations. Any use of a person’s voice or reuse of recordings would need separate permission, applicable rights checks and speaker review.

Pathway 05 of 10

Control Over Data And Digital Work

The CARE principles connect Indigenous data governance with collective benefit, authority to control, responsibility and ethics. FNIGC describes ownership, control, access and possession through the First Nations principles of OCAP®. FNIGC is explicit that these are specifically principles of First Nations in Canada; each Nation’s own protocols remain essential.

Prepare A Brief Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Read The Governance Frameworks

Use the cited CARE paper and FNIGC’s own explanation before selecting a tool. These are external learning resources. ZOTHEOS has not been certified against them or endorsed by their authors. OCAP® is a registered trademark of the First Nations Information Governance Centre (FNIGC).

Work You Control

Write The Rules Before Gathering Data

Decide who authorizes collection, who benefits, who can see or change records, where copies sit, how retention works and who can stop use. Separate public research from citizenship files, health records, restricted knowledge and precise sensitive locations. Write down permitted uses and a contact route for correction or withdrawal.

Proposed App Workflow

Test A Community-Controlled Workspace

A proposed local workflow could help organize permitted material under access and retention rules that the community chooses. It would require verified security, export, deletion and network behavior. Local storage alone does not establish consent, collective control, confidentiality or protection from a compromised device.

Pathway 06 of 10

Children, Families And Community Connections

NICWA explains the Indian Child Welfare Act as a US law with defined requirements for covered child-custody proceedings. Its application involves specific criteria; it is not a universal rule for every family or country. A useful guide helps someone reach the right specialist while protecting the child’s privacy.

Prepare A Brief Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Reach The Original Guidance

Open NICWA’s explanation and family resources from its own site. This page provides a reading route, not case intake or an eligibility finding. A documentary can give historical context, but cannot establish what should happen in an individual proceeding.

Work You Control

Prepare For A Qualified Conversation

If a US child-welfare matter may involve ICWA, speak promptly with the child’s Tribe and a qualified attorney or legal service, as NICWA advises. Privately organize notices, dates, questions and existing case contacts. Ask how the relevant law, Tribal membership determination and current court process apply; avoid posting identifying records publicly.

Proposed App Workflow

Keep Any Draft Assistance Narrow

A possible ZOTHEOS workflow could help a person organize questions from documents they are authorized to use. It would require rigorous privacy and accuracy checks. It must not decide membership, legal eligibility, custody, placement or the best interests of a child, and it must not delay professional help.

Pathway 09 of 10

Worldwide Rights, Local Routes

UNDRIP offers a worldwide rights framework, including cross-border relationships in Article 36. Practical routes vary by place. The UN’s legal commentary discusses its influence and relationships with other law; a declaration, a domestic statute, a treaty and a court judgment should not be treated as interchangeable.

Prepare A Brief Sources And Further Reading

Website Now

Start With The Right People And Place

Use worldwide directory navigation to find the distinct people, Nation or representative organization. Then read its own sources. Country and region labels help browsing; they do not define identity, exclusive homelands or who may speak for a community.

Work You Control

Find The Applicable Process

Identify the decision, the relevant jurisdiction and the community-authorized institution or adviser. Ask which domestic, regional, treaty or international process is actually available, with what requirements and risks. A general rights statement does not by itself establish an enforceable remedy, an admissible complaint or a guaranteed response.

Proposed App Workflow

Compare Without Flattening Differences

A proposed ZOTHEOS workflow could compare public accounts across borders while retaining original language, dates and separate legal contexts. It would need reviewed translations and local expertise. It should not turn another Nation’s agreement, legal status or practice into a universal template.

Something You Can Prepare

Proposed App Workflow

Track Promises Against Evidence

A future workflow could compare a public commitment with dated records of what followed. People would choose the measures and decide whether publication is appropriate. A polished report, a meeting or a positive sentiment score must not be substituted for a demonstrated change in authority, access or conditions.

Hypothetical Planning Exercise · Work You Control

Try This With The Evidence

Hypothetical reader question: How could I distinguish a completed land transaction from later plans and check what a public restoration announcement actually establishes?

  1. Read the Yurok Tribe's July 28, 2026 land-transfer announcement. Start a private ledger with separate columns for the statement, the event date, the publication date, the evidence and what remains unknown. Record the June 30 conveyance separately from the August 1 opening date announced in the release.
  2. Separate ownership, operation, restoration work, public access and future construction into different rows. Label each row as a reported completed action, an announced plan or a question needing evidence. A future cultural-center plan is not a completed building; a scheduled opening is not a record of later access.
  3. For a public commitment you are studying, name one observable change and the public record that could demonstrate it. Preserve the original statement and date, then add any later source as a new entry. If no later record has been checked, write that limitation instead of marking success or failure.
  4. Finish a one-page commitment ledger with the responsible institution, evidence gaps and your next review date. Use it to prepare a precise question for qualified human review; keep sensitive places and private community information out of the public note.

What This Does Not Establish This is a reading and evidence-organizing exercise, not a property-title search, a legal finding, an audit of the Tribe or permission to visit, collect material or act for the rights holders. The release is the Tribe's public account; it does not establish that every planned benefit later occurred or that another land claim would have the same route or outcome.

Roots and gratitude

ZOTHEOS draws its visual language from ancient Mesoamerica: the stepped fret, the fine black line of the painted codices, the count of days. It honors the Indigenous peoples of the Americas: the Nahua, Maya, Zapotec and many other peoples of Mexico and Central America, and the Native nations further north. They are distinct peoples with connected roots that are still living, and many Mexican and Mexican American families carry those roots too. As the project grows, it intends to work with Indigenous scholars and community members, so that every symbol here is used accurately and with permission. Any wider work with the traditions of other peoples is meant to follow the same rule: their guidance, their permission. That work has a first page: Indigenous nations today, their own names first, every fact with its source and the date it was checked.
